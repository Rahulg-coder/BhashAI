# SIH 2026 Problem Statement SIH26042 Context

## Problem Title
**AI-powered Vernacular Pedagogy and Real-Time Translation Application for Mother Tongue-Based Multilingual Education (MTB-MLE).**

## Geographic & Educational Scope
- **State**: Jharkhand, India.
- **Target Learners**: Primary school students (Grades 1 to 3 / Foundational Stage) belonging to tribal communities.
- **Languages**: Primary prototype in **Santhali** (using the official **Ol Chiki** script), with modular roadmap for **Mundari** and **Ho**.
- **Pedagogical Alignment**: National Initiative for Proficiency in Reading with Understanding and Numeracy (**NIPUN Bharat**) / Foundational Literacy and Numeracy (**FLN**).

## Why Generic Translators Fail
1. **Lack of Educational Context**: Generic translators do not know if "जोड़" means addition or joining, or whether a sentence is a teacher instruction or student assessment question.
2. **Terminology Fluctuation**: Generic translators randomly switch between synonyms across sentences in the same lesson, confusing young learners.
3. **Low-Resource Language Inaccuracies**: Tribal languages have limited NLP training corpora, frequently producing inaccurate or culturally inappropriate text.
4. **Internet Dependency**: Primary tribal schools frequently experience low or zero connectivity, requiring complete offline operational capabilities on low-cost hardware (2 GB RAM Android tablets).

## The BhashAI Differentiator
BhashAI directly addresses all 4 failure modes through context-aware translation, an approved educational terminology glossary, validation safeguards, and offline-first Room/SQLite synchronization.
